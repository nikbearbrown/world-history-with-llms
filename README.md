## About the Author

**Nik Bear Brown** teaches at Northeastern University. His work runs across computer science, computational biology, AI, education, and the broader question of how human institutions adapt to powerful new tools — questions a world-historical lens treats as its core material.

He is the founder of [Humanitarians AI](https://www.humanitarians.ai/), the author of *Irreducibly Human*, and the author of the *with LLMs* textbook series. His PhD in Computer Science is from UCLA.

He works in Boston. [nikbearbrown.com](https://www.nikbearbrown.com) · [irreducibly.xyz](https://irreducibly.xyz) · [skepticism.ai](https://www.skepticism.ai)
